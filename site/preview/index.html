    Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

    Is TrackFinder free?

    Yes. TrackFinder is free to download and use for personal use. No account, no subscription.

    Does it work on Windows and Mac?

    Yes. It runs on Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) and Windows 11, and on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

    Where do the tracks come from?

    TrackFinder reads the public tracklists on 1001Tracklists for the DJ and period you choose, then merges them into one list. TrackFinder is not affiliated with 1001Tracklists.

    Which DJs can I search?

    Any artist whose sets have tracklists published on 1001Tracklists. Type their name, pick a period, and TrackFinder does the digging.

    What happens to IDs and repeated tracks?

    By default they are left out, so you get each track once. If you want them, turn on "Include IDs and unreleased" before you search.

    Can I listen to the tracks and keep the list?

    Tracks that are linked on Spotify or SoundCloud get a shortcut to listen there. You can save the whole list as a .txt file, one "Artist – Title" per line.

    Can I use it on my phone?

    TrackFinder is a desktop app for Windows and macOS. On your phone, send yourself the link and download it on your computer.
